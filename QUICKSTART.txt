DAILY SILVER 2.0 — SUMMIT EDITION

UPDATING EXISTING GAME:
1. Copy the files from daily-silver-2-0-update-files.zip over your current project.
2. Keep assets/sprites/crystal/front/ and back/ — they are NOT included in the ZIP.
3. Run PLAY-ON-WINDOWS.bat (Node.js 18+). Refresh browser with Ctrl+Shift+R.

NEW SOURCE COPY:
1. Extract daily-silver-2-0-full-source.zip.
2. Run PLAY-ON-WINDOWS.bat to download necessary Crystal sprites once.
3. The browser opens at http://localhost:4182 . Then everything works offline.

OPTIONAL COMPLETE NATIONAL DEX (251):
  Run EXPAND-TO-251-WINDOWS.bat once with internet; generates offline roster
  from PokéAPI and downloads additional Crystal artwork. Not included by default.

GAME:
  Arrow-free menu; 1–4 attacks, P opens party, press SKIP FX for rapid play.
  OPTIONS controls animation speed and volume. Hall of Fame includes replay.

No database, no PHP, no subscriptions. Personal, unofficial fan project.
Read README.md for mechanics and intellectual-property limitations.
